What happens at the gate

First visit: the worker does the ELISE Constructions site induction on their phone, about 10 minutes. It covers their details, emergency contact, health, white card and tickets, the 32 acknowledgement items and a signature. It comes to Approvals for you. They only do it once, and it's good for 12 months.

Every day: scan, sign in, read and sign their company's SWMS when there's a new one, and sign out. Anyone left signed in at 6pm is signed out automatically.

From the same page they can request a permit or register plant. Both come to you for approval.

Add ELISE to your home screen

Do it once. Then sign in every day with one tap.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. 1Open the sign-in in Safari

    Scan the QR code at the gate with your camera. It opens in Safari.

  2. 2Tap the Share button

    The square with the arrow pointing up. On the newest iPhones tap ••• first, then Share.

  3. 3Tap “Add to Home Screen”

    Scroll down the list if you can't see it.

  4. 4Tap “Add”

    Top right corner. Leave the name as it is.

  5. 5Done - tap ELISE every day

    The ELISE icon is on your home screen. It opens straight to the sign-in, even with no signal.

Android (Chrome)

  1. 1Open the sign-in in Chrome

    Scan the QR code at the gate with your camera. Open it in Chrome.

  2. 2Tap the three dots ⋮

    Top right corner of Chrome.

  3. 3Tap “Add to home screen”

    Some phones say Install app instead - tap that.

  4. 4Tap “Add” or “Install”

    If it asks again, tap Add once more.

  5. 5Done - tap ELISE every day

    The ELISE icon is on your home screen. It opens straight to the sign-in, even with no signal.

Can't see the option? Make sure the page is open in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android - not inside WhatsApp, Facebook or another app. If it opened inside another app, tap its menu and choose “Open in browser”.

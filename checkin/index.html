How to sign in
  1. First time on siteDo the ELISE Constructions induction on this page - about 10 minutes, once only. Elise approves it.
  2. Every dayScan the gate QR, tap SIGN IN, read and sign today's site prestart, and your company's SWMS when there's a new one.
  3. Leaving siteTap SIGN OUT. Anyone still signed in at 6pm is signed out automatically.
  4. CrewA foreman can sign the whole crew in with MY CREW - each person still does their own induction once.
  5. No signalIt still works. It saves on your phone and sends when you're back on the network.
  6. Permits & plantRequest a permit or register plant from the tiles. Elise approves it and you see it here.
